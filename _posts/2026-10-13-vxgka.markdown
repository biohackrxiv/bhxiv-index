BioHackrXiv continues to host project reports from biohackathon events (Prins et al., 2022).
It has published more than 150 preprints and are indexed by Europe PMC and notable preprints
are indexed by Wikidata too. It has supported multiple biohackathons, including the DBCLS
BioHackathon and BioHackathon Europe series (Garcia et al., 2020). A full overview of
supported meetings is available from the index.biohackrxiv.org website. The original Markdown
setup with metadata in YAML headers (Perk et al., 2024) has been extended since 2020 with
support for citation intent annotations with the Citation Typing Ontology (Willighagen et al.,
2023), making ORCID identifiers for authors and ROR identifiers for research organizations
visible in the PDFs (Willighagen &amp;amp; Ohta, 2025), and support for the Contributor Role Taxonomy
(CRediT) annotation of author roles (McNutt et al., 2018). These things have been developed
with the help of the same communities BioHackrXiv supports.
