For the life science semantic web community, the provision of genomic variants ready for tools and applications
is essential to deliver on the promise of precision medicine. As part of the DBCLS BioHackathon 2026, we here
report our efforts on how best to provide variant data in RDF for downstream patient-clinical trial matching
applications.
